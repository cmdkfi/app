## 3. Scripted liquidation on the demo market

The victim was the wallet's demo position from section 1: 0.003 WETH collateral, 3.510793 dUSDC debt. The
liquidator was the same wallet, which Morpho allows (self-liquidation).

**Price needed.** Morpho liquidates when debt > collateral × price × LLTV. So the position was liquidatable below
3.510793 / (0.003 × 0.86) = $1,360.77. Below 3.510793 × LIF / 0.003 = $1,221.57 the collateral would not cover the
seizure, and Morpho would book bad debt (LIF = 1 / (1 − 0.3 × 0.14) = 1.043841). Chosen: $1,340.00, where the LTV is
3.510793 / 4.02 = 0.8733.

**Price move down.** `rebalance_pool.py --market demo --set-price 1340` took 98 transactions:
- It burnt the sell and buy bands.
- It made 30 steps of at most 1.9%, each a limit-priced swap through the anchor followed by `MockOracle.setPrice`. The first step was $2,296.06 (0x947cc84a86a4dad7d5481593b1bf7490ec32b81857d03c096595edc49d98522b, block 53,817,424) and the last was $1,340.00 (0x43342cc7795db3abf76bac0bba9f2122e495db7f25837642afc9ec686d4415e0, block 53,818,950).
- It minted the new bands before the last `setPrice`.

The swaps took in 0.001597 WETH in total, and the fee was 0.000035981 ETH.

**Dry run** (nothing sent), reading at $1,340.00: max borrow 3.457200 < debt 3.510793; plan "repay all shares";
repay 3.510793 dUSDC; seize 0.002734858208955223 WETH (worth 3.664710 dUSDC at the oracle). By hand, 3.510793 × 1.043841 / 1340 = 0.00273486 WETH.

**Liquidation.**

## Re-read the positions

Each command prints `supplyShares, borrowShares, collateral` (Morpho `position`). Remove `--block` to read the current
state. Debt = borrowShares × (totalBorrowAssets + 1) / (totalBorrowShares + 10⁶), rounded up, from
`market(bytes32)(uint128,uint128,uint128,uint128,uint128,uint128)`.

```sh

LIVE=0xa25ac5171b7e500b82653d3750b91bb44e2409c32ca3c9e80b0db077e8a4535c

cast call $MORPHO "$P" $LIVE   $W --block 53817177 --rpc-url $RPC   # live before the move: 3549999450000, 3372448000000, 3000000000000000
```
