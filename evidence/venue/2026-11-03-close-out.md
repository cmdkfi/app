`--close-bands` is new (3 Nov). It enumerates every NonfungiblePositionManager token the wallet holds
(`balanceOf`, `tokenOfOwnerByIndex`). It keeps those on this market's pool (token pair and 0.01% fee), and
`decreaseLiquidity` + `collect`s each one that holds liquidity or owed tokens. It then writes `{}` to the state file. It does
no swap, no re-mint and no budget check. The dry runs listed the same four positions and amounts as the real runs.
