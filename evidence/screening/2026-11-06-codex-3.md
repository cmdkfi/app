## Discrepancies

1. **The floor guarantee is too absolute.**  
   README says the agent never sells below the floor. `reduce` calculates repayment assuming an oracle-price fill, then permits a better fill without checking final LTV. For example, collateral value 1,000, debt 400, floor 30%, and a 1%-better fill yield approximately **29.9506% LTV** after the calculated repayment, within a 20% budget. This is a code/arithmetic finding, not a newly executed transaction.

2. **The target wording is internally inconsistent.**  
   README’s invariant says protective selling “never sells past target,” while its limitations correctly disclose undershoot. The implementation also allows stopping within **0.1 percentage points above target**. The live protective example ends at **40.01515%** against a 40% target. The precise guarantee needs the tolerance and execution-price qualification throughout.

3. **The NEAR slice maximum applies specifically to `reduce`.**  
   `vault/src/lib.rs:439` checks `slice_max`; `migrate` bounds a fraction and `exit` closes the position without that amount check. Broad permission-table wording can imply the slice cap covers all three methods.

4. **“Widest case” in the incident-replay narrative is incorrect.**  
   README names 136 versus 25 minutes. Its cited report, `research/judge-evidence/incident-replay-results.md:174`, also reports **5.7 versus 3.5 days**, a larger absolute lead-time improvement. The narrowest stated improvement of 14 minutes agrees with that report.

5. **Exit timing mixes publication timestamp and push time.**  
   The evidence gives item timestamp **16:22:08**, push **16:23:10**, and mined exit **16:29:09**. That is **7m01s from the embedded timestamp**, but **5m59s after the push**, rather than seven minutes after the push.

6. **Recipient language is shorthand, not literal token-flow behavior.**  
   Collateral pays the fixed swap pool (`Executor.sol:429`), and the owner can withdraw gas ETH with `sweep_eth` (`vault/src/lib.rs:375`). Thus “only to the caller” and “the only way money leaves is withdraw” should describe the intended prohibition on **agent-selected recipients** more precisely. The inspected agent methods do preserve that intended restriction.

7. **Some commands could not be executed literally within this run.**  
   The attack command and sandbox test suite conflict with the explicit no-signing/no-transactions rules. Native cargo-near builds hit a protected configuration-write failure. These are recorded separately from product failures; the equivalent vault-container build and adjusted agent builds passed.

8. **Research consistency was checked, but full research reproduction remains incomplete.**
   - Morpho rows sum to **20,479** liquidations.
   - Published size-filtered tables contain **1,890/1,895** and **5,009/5,011**.
   - The grid contains 162 alternatives; its best liquidation count is 853 versus the reported default’s 801.
   - All **155 locally available manifest files** matched their hashes. The public release lists **17 assets totaling 1,968,184,899 bytes**.
   - I did not download/replay the entire dataset or regenerate the model experiments. The **20/20 survival, $2.65M penalties avoided, thin-buffer keeper results, incident lead times, injection rates and repeatability figures** therefore remain reported research findings rather than independently reproduced outcomes.
   - The notes disclose unavailable internal replay inputs/scripts, including `backtest_scripts/incident_replay_llm.py` and incident/model-run collections. Older planning paths are not runnable from this checkout.

9. **Other numerical claims remain unconfirmed independently.**  
   This includes the idealized BTC/recovery examples, mainnet cost estimates, the implied scaling economics, mainnet signer count, external borrower/TVL/market-size figures, and cited third-party incident losses. The cost note assumes an untested capacity estimate at **five-minute checks**, while this agent targets roughly **two-second ticks**; it does not establish production cost at the implemented cadence.

No missing literal file path was found among the README references checked, including all verification evidence files. Production-only Intents/privacy, absent live `open_from_stables` evidence, the missing outage backstop, and the NEAR `PENDING` broadcast problem are **disclosed limitations**, not hidden discrepancies.
