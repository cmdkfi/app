## Discrepancies

1. **Floor wording is broader than the implemented guarantee.**  
   The live landing’s [machine-readable description](https://cmdk.fi/llms-full.txt) says the agent cannot sell past the floor. This comes from **landing** `src/content/landing.ts:820`. Similar unqualified wording appears in **web** `app/src/screens/Settings.tsx:26` and the root README’s floor row. The floor constrains reduction sizing; an authorized `exit` repays all debt and closes the market without applying that floor (`Executor.sol:360`). The root README’s detailed worst-case explanation correctly distinguishes exit. Public copy should make the distinction equally explicit.

2. **Some daily-budget wording omits exemptions.**  
   **web** `README.md:29` summarizes an absolute maximum of 20% sold per rolling day; landing corridor copy is similarly broad. Protective sessions bypass that budget, and authorized exit has its own permission rather than that budget. The app’s detailed explanatory text correctly describes the protective-session exemption. The historical automatic exit sold approximately **50.17%** of its pre-exit collateral. This is intended contract behavior, not a demonstrated enforcement bypass.

3. **“Same debt” migration shorthand omits rounding.**  
   **landing** `src/content/landing.ts:813` says migration preserves the same debt. The implementation permits the documented small rounding increase, up to two loan-token units. Other landing copy, the main README, and the app disclose the **0.000002 USDC** allowance. The wording is inconsistent.

4. **Venue-split output files are missing despite being advertised.**  
   `research/README.md:12` promises scripts “and their outputs”; `research/venue-split/venue_split_gap_risk.md:6` names `scripts/out/`. That directory and referenced outputs such as `B_crossings.csv`, `B_whipsaw.csv`, `C_aggregate.csv`, and `tables.md` are absent. The note and six Python files are present. The root Verify-it paths themselves existed.

5. **Research headline counts require their cohort qualifications.**  
   The reproduced **1,890/1,895** and **5,009/5,011** figures apply to qualifying observed-liquidation cases, with size/data-quality filters. They do not describe every position. The detailed dataset documentation also reports **12 observed-survivor positions liquidating in the simulation**—nine training, three holdout. “Defaults beat every alternative” was supported by the supplied liquidation-count results, not established across every economic metric.

6. **Several numerical claims were not independently regenerated.**  
   These remain reported results or estimates, rather than newly reproduced findings:
   - The 20-position backtest’s **$2.65 million** avoided penalties; its complete original scripts/raw inputs are not shipped. The missing `rpc.py` helper is explicitly disclosed in `research/README.md:16`.
   - The **837 days**, **9.9%/16.0%** maximum drops and **53-minute** act-to-liquidation result; the minute-data replay was not rerun.
   - The incident replay’s detection and lead-time improvements; supplied results were inspected, but paid model calls were not repeated.
   - Infrastructure **$0.70–1.00 per vault/month** and **2–3 cents/action**; these are estimates, and the README explicitly excludes pricing the current two-second ticks.
   - Web performance figures such as **0.44–0.70 seconds** per refresh and **1.8 seconds** to first full position view; the functional request-budget tests passed, but those historical timings were not reproduced under controlled conditions.
   - Business/competitor/incident figures, including borrower counts, market sizes, liquidation totals, competitor assets/positions, and historical loss amounts. They were not independently corroborated in this screening.

7. **Some assurances necessarily remain narrower than a full live demonstration.**  
   No fresh live wallet onboarding, closing, revocation, or `open_from_stables` transaction was sent. `open_from_stables` has code/tests but no live evidence transaction, as disclosed. Browser tests use fixture wallets and services; real-device mobile signing remains pending in the web README. The transcript samples establish recorded-message attribution, not independent re-verification of the model’s complete TDX quote. Uptime and renewal behavior were sampled/read, not observed for days.

8. **Failed initial commands were environmental or documented prerequisites.**  
   The initial Foundry run lacked the documented submodule; it passed after initialization in a temporary clone. Buildx permissions, Chromium sandbox restrictions, emulation timeouts and the constrained Node heap required the adaptations described above. They did not remain unresolved product failures. No source fix was needed.

The submission clearly labels its major unbuilt features—Confidential Intents, any-chain funding, BTC support, additional venues, buy-back, and an independent emergency backstop—as roadmap work. They should not receive implementation credit, but their absence is not a concealed discrepancy.

## Preliminary score: **8.5 / 10**

Reasons supporting the score:

- Substantial, working NEAR integration: scoped protocol credentials, on-chain attestation checks, Global Contracts, owner signatures and Chain Signatures.
- Unusually strong artifact reproducibility: matching deployed vault, matching factory build, two matching agent builds, and matching deployed web bundles.
- Broad passing tests and independently checked live receipts, including deliberately unsuccessful cases.
- A functional web app with recovery paths, policy checks, evidence displays and a clearly isolated sandbox.

Reasons it is below 10:

- Public floor/budget wording needs correction.
- The incremental benefit of the model is less firmly established than the deterministic protection mechanism.
- Some research is only reproducible from supplied result files; other advertised outputs are missing.
- Fresh live wallet interoperability was not exercised, and deployment remains a single-venue testnet system with disclosed signing-path and availability dependencies.

This score assesses the submission as a whole. It is **preliminary screening evidence, not a rejection mechanism or security certification**.
