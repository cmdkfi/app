## Questions for the human judges

1. The factory's keylessness is procedural (keys deleted after deployment) and the factory wasm was host-built, so its NEP-330 metadata has no source link; the on-chain state (no keys, fixed hash) is what the argument rests on. Is that acceptable for "nobody, including us, can … create vaults with other code"?
2. One shared enclave serves every vault, and the owner approves a measurement, not an operator. How should judges weigh TEE.fail-style attacks (the README itself calls the enclave defence in depth) against the corridor bounds as the real limit?
3. The model's contribution in the live build is one `migrate` under venue stress and one text-path `exit` on the team's own demo feeds, with a judge that answers differently in about a third of identical repeats. Is the AI component weighted as a decision aid inside hard bounds (as designed) or expected to show more lift?
4. Protection has no backstop in v1: if NEAR, Chain Signatures (11 of 16 operators), the relayer or the agent is down, nothing protects the position. Does the roadmap's permissionless `protect` need to exist before the "around the clock" claim is judged?
5. The private funding route (Confidential Intents) and any-chain deposits are product claims with no testnet; the hackathon build takes WETH directly on Base Sepolia. How much of the Privacy track score should rest on what is demonstrated (attested inference, signed transcripts) versus what is specified?
6. The README's prior-art figure for DeFi Saver (7-day timelock, 3-of-6 multisig) has no note in `research/`; judges may want the team to point to its source.
7. Transcripts, including the model's reasoning about a user's position, are public in this build (decided 5 Nov, private in production). Is that acceptable for a privacy-track entry at the testnet stage?
8. The vault's `cancel` relies on the relayer to pick the pending Base nonce and owner messages expire within an hour; both are unstated operational details. Should the team document the relayer's trust boundary for owner actions more explicitly?
