What did not happen, and why the README's earlier wording was wrong: the agent made no call after the revoke, so there is no
`AccessKeyNotFound` in any log. The agent re-reads the vault's state every refresh and stops the vault's loop when it no longer
approves the measurement (`logic::agent_status`, `may_propose` false when revoked). Had it tried, the NEAR protocol would refuse
the signature with `AccessKeyNotFound`, because the key is gone from the account (0 keys above); that refusal is at the
protocol, not in our code. A held signature past its deadline fails on Base with `Expired`: shown from the wallet path in
evidence/attacks.txt (attack Expired, selector 0x203d82d8).
