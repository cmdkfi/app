## What stays internal, and what we will do with it

The notes here are the findings. The material behind them is ours and is not published:

- **The liquidation dataset's raw cache and training runs:** the derived tables of the 20,479 Morpho Blue liquidations from 2024 to October 2026 (hourly prices, position histories, replay results) and the scripts that build and replay them are published since 6 November 2026: files under 5 MB in `liquidation-dataset/data/`, larger ones as assets of the GitHub release `research-data-2026-11-06` (1.97 GB; list, checksums and download command in `liquidation-dataset/DATA.md`). What stays internal is the 10 GB cache of the public API pulls (`data/raw/`, regenerable by the scripts), the 908 MB of per-policy grid-search runs and the intermediate pull state; none of it is needed to recompute the README's numbers. We will use the dataset to tune the corridor defaults per market for the production release (the grid search in `liquidation-dataset/README.md` is the first pass) and to retrain the risk model we measured and set aside (it added no alerts to the fixed screens on the 2026 holdout).
- **The model runs:** the runs `judge-evidence/README.md` cites are published since 10 November 2026 in `judge-evidence/runs/` (the requests, responses and errors of the five control groups and the incident set, the step results and summaries: 2,617 files, 63 MB). Exploratory runs the note does not cite stay internal. The published set is also our regression set: a change to the judge's prompt, model or agreement rule is replayed against it before it ships.
- **The backtest inputs:** the sampled positions behind `liquidation-backtest/backtest.md` and the scripts other than `fetch_aave_liqs.py`; the hourly and minute Binance prices are published with the liquidation dataset (`liquidation-backtest/backtest_data/`, see `liquidation-dataset/DATA.md`).

References in the notes to those files are kept as plain text and marked "internal file". Judges who want to check a number against data not published here can ask; we will share the relevant extract.
